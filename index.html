🎯 What This Mod Does

Have you ever played Cyberpunk 2077 in third-person mode and noticed V walks sideways or backwards while your camera points forward? That's frustrating, right? This mod fixes exactly that problem.

With cyberpunk2077-tcoc, V will automatically rotate to face the direction you're moving. If you press forward, V runs forward. If you strafe left, V turns to face left while moving. It's natural, immersive, and makes third-person gameplay feel like a proper action game.

This mod is designed specifically as an add-on for Immersive Third Person, so it works together with that popular mod to give you the best third-person experience possible.
